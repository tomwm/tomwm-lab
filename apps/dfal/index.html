DFAL

Drummer From Another Lab: an analogue-style percussion synth and 8-step sequencer, with a patch bay that reaches other synth windows.

Steps

Each step sets a pitch (semitones; the knobs span about ten octaves, so small moves go a long way) and a velocity. Velocity sets the level of all three envelopes and slightly lengthens their decays, so 0 is a rest. Drag a knob up or down (Shift for fine), scroll, or use the arrow keys; double-click to reset. Space runs and stops.

Patch bay

Click an output then an input (or the other way round) to patch a cable. Click the same pair again, or the cable itself, to remove it. Alt-click a jack to pull all its cables. Outputs have a filled label. Patching Trigger, Velocity, Ext audio or VCF mod breaks its normal connection; the other inputs add to their knobs, as on the hardware.

Other windows

Synths open in other windows of this browser appear here. Click a jack here and one on this window's patch bay to join them, or arm a jack in one window and finish the cable in the other. Control voltages, gates, pitch and audio all cross between windows (audio can crackle if a window is busy). A window that sends signals delays its own sound by 50 ms so both stay in time.
