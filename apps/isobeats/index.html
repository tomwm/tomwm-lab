    Sequence moves

    Tune each oscillator, then choose what the step pitches move. Both plays tuned drums; Osc 2 keeps a fixed body under a moving click; Off makes the pattern all velocity. The weather still sets each oscillator's wave, the FM between them, the pitch drop and the mix.

    Each step has a pitch and a velocity. Low pitches with a long drop are kicks, middle ones toms, high ones blips. Velocity 0 is a rest, and steps under 50% are quiet steps that the moon thins out. The weather only adds to steps you play: with every step at 0 it is silent. Drag a knob up or down (Shift for fine), scroll, or use the arrow keys; double-click to reset. Space runs and stops; Z and X transpose. A MIDI note transposes the pattern; when stopped, it also triggers the current step.
