## How it works
### 1. Answer a few questions
We ask a small number of relevant questions about timing, symptoms, exposure, and context.

### 2. Get a clear risk summary
You receive a structured summary with plain-language guidance and appropriate next steps.

### 3. Explore your edge cases
You can then chat with Sylvan to better understand uncertainty, nuance, and what to do next.

## Trust section
Sexual Elf is designed to make sexual health guidance feel easier to understand.
It is:
- evidence-led
- private by design
- non-judgmental
- educational, not a medical diagnosis

## Disclaimer
Sexual Elf provides educational information only and does not replace professional medical advice, testing, diagnosis, or treatment.
