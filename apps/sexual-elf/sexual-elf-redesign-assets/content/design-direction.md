# Sexual Elf design direction

## Positioning
Sexual Elf should feel like a calm, private, evidence-led digital guide for sexual health decision support.

## Visual principles
- Editorial rather than cartoon
- Soft, muted palette
- Clean linework
- Rounded but not childish shapes
- Trust before delight
- Symbolic guide rather than mascot

## Sylvan character direction
Sylvan should feel:
- intelligent
- calm
- discreet
- non-judgmental
- slightly abstract

Avoid:
- anime styling
- oversized eyes
- glossy rendering
- fantasy game aesthetics
- overt flirtation
- childish cuteness

## Logo direction
The logo should remain unchanged in principle:
- symmetrical
- organic
- elf-like
- subtly anatomical
- elegant, not explicit
