Brother 32

An analogue-style mono synth with a 32-step sequencer and a patch bay that reaches other synth windows. Patch it to DFAL.

Octave 0

Sequencer

Click a step to select it. Its gate button cycles On → Tie (held into the next step) → Rest. A accents it, G glides into it, ×1 repeats it up to four times. Scroll on a note or use the arrow keys to change it, or turn on Step entry and play the keyboard. Keys A to ; play two octaves (W, E, T, Y, U, O, P are the black keys). Space runs and stops.

Patch bay

Click an output then an input (or the other way round) to patch a cable. Click the same pair again, or the cable itself, to remove it. Alt-click a jack to pull all its cables. Outputs have a filled label. Patching an input breaks its normal connection, as on hardware.

Other windows

Synths open in other windows of this browser appear here. Click a jack here and one on this window's patch bay to join them, or arm a jack in one window and finish the cable in the other. Control voltages, gates, pitch and audio all cross between windows (audio can crackle if a window is busy). A window that sends signals delays its own sound by 50 ms so both stay in time.
